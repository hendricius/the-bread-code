My mission is simple: to teach people around the world how to make amazing bread at home. No expensive equipment needed. Just flour, water, salt, and a little patience.

Why I Created The Bread Code

Have you ever relished the taste of a fresh, warm bread with a crispy crust? Do you know the sound of the crispy crust when you take a bite? Have you ever experienced the delicious, homey scent of bread coming right out of the oven?

When baking bread, you experience a whole variety of emotions and sensations. You step into a world where simplicity can become beautifully complex. The feeling of having crafted a perfect combination of all natural ingredients is unique and special.

There is no such thing as "the perfect bread" — every bread you make is unique, and that's the beauty of it.

You Can Start Today!

10,000 years ago, people baked bread without any fancy tools. All you really need is:

  • A bowl
  • Flour, water, salt
  • A heat source (your oven works great!)
  • A bit of patience and curiosity

The Programmer's Approach to Baking

Being a programmer myself, I lacked scientific resources on bread baking. In my daytime job, we use A/B testing for almost everything. Why not apply the same proven methodologies to baking?

🧪

A/B Test

Change one variable at a time. Document what works and what doesn't. Every experiment teaches you something new.

🔄

Iterate

Each bread teaches you something. Apply your learnings to the next bake. You will fail sometimes — and that's okay!

📝

Document

Keep notes. Share your experiments. Help others learn from your journey. That's what this community is all about.
